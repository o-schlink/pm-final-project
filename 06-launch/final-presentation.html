One-line pitch

Slide 5 · Strategy

Problem, Value & Hypothesis

Module 1 + 3

Problem statement hook

Responsible Payers often cannot clearly tell whether an installment plan covers the full treatment cost, a single invoice, a co-payment, or a remaining balance. This creates post-creation clarification needs and makes BFS’s installment offering harder to scale across verticals without bespoke configurations.

Value proposition

For Responsible Payers, the Financing Basis Summary explains the financed amount, exclusions, monthly payment, duration, and total repayment before confirmation. For BFS, this creates a reusable clarity pattern that supports scalable vertical expansion without redesigning risk or regulatory foundations.

Data-backed hypothesis

Based on qualitative evidence from Responsible Payers and quantitative evidence that 46.2K of 316K agreements, around 14.6%, have post-creation manual contact, we believe that adding one pre-confirmation Financing Basis Summary screen will reduce manual contact by improving payer understanding. Success will be measured by a synthetic target of 2 percentage points absolute reduction, from 14.6% to 12.6%, while protecting activation rate and payment health.

Slide 6 · Research

Status Quo Is The Competitor

Module 2

Competitive analysis / workaround

The real competitor is the status quo: Responsible Payers rely on informal handoffs, phone calls, emails, forwarded links, invoices, treatment plans and partner explanations to understand what they are paying for. This workaround is fragile because the payer, service recipient and decision maker may differ, and because treatment costs, invoices, co-payments and reimbursements do not always happen at the same time.

Moment of misery

The payer cannot confidently identify what they are committing to before confirmation.

The journey breaks when real-world roles, invoices, reimbursements and cost timing do not match the standard product flow.

Roadmap rationale

NOW: C2 Financing Basis Summary, C3 Payment Commitment Preview and C12 Self-Service Funnel & Contact Reason Tracking. These test the core assumption: clearer pre-confirmation information reduces post-creation manual contact. NEXT: plan-change impact preview, returned direct-debit resolution and partner-context communication. LATER: larger data-model, plan-management and exception-tracking work. CUT from pilot: partner prefill and configurable rule-engine build, because they risk turning the pilot into a platform rebuild before the clarity assumption is validated.

PRD highlights

The prototype focuses on one pre-confirmation Financing Basis Summary screen. It shows the financed amount, financing basis type, included and excluded amounts, monthly payment, duration and total repayment before confirmation. It also highlights payer vs. service recipient, future exclusions, and help intent. Out of scope: eligibility logic, risk decisioning, partner integration, contract generation, payment collection and adding later invoices to an active plan.

Slide 8 · Validation

Experiment Plan

Module 5

Hypothesis

A/B test one added pre-confirmation Financing Basis Summary screen against the current confirmation experience. Primary metric: post-creation manual contact rate within 30 days after agreement creation. Baseline: 14.6% overall, equal to 46.2K of 316K agreements. Synthetic MDE: 2 percentage point absolute reduction, from 14.6% to 12.6%.

Guardrails and decision rules

  • Guardrails: agreement completion / activation rate must not drop by more than 1 percentage point, and payment health must not worsen, measured by direct debit return rate and extension rate.
  • Diagnostic metric: correct understanding rate and contact reasons tagged as financing-basis confusion.
  • Ship only if the contact-rate reduction is significant, guardrails remain healthy, and diagnostic evidence supports clarity as the driver.

Slide 9 · Launch

GTM Strategy & Success Dashboard

Module 6

GTM strategy

Launch goal: Conversion. This is a targeted M-tier experiment for Responsible Payers in the installment confirmation flow, supported by internal enablement for Customer Support, partner-facing teams and selected pilot partners. Channels: in-flow product communication, internal enablement note, and partner pilot briefing. The launch avoids broad awareness activity because the feature first needs to prove that clearer pre-confirmation information reduces post-creation manual contact.

Slide 10 · Story

What Changed In The Thinking

Reflection

Friction points + aha moment

The hardest part was separating real evidence from attractive AI-generated structure. AI helped us move fast, but it also tended to smooth over important tensions: payer clarity vs. partner workflow, vertical-specific needs vs. scalable core, and contact reduction vs. healthy activation. The aha moment was realizing that the best first move is not the biggest platform solution, but a focused test of the clearest pain point: whether Responsible Payers understand what they are committing to before confirmation.

Key takeaways / next

Key takeaway: good product work is less about generating features and more about making assumptions testable. Next, I would validate whether financing-basis confusion is truly a major driver of post-creation contact by combining the A/B test with comprehension checks and contact-reason tagging. If validated, I would scale the clarity pattern first, then decide which deeper platform capabilities are justified.
