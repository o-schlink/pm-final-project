Slide 5 · Strategy

Make financing simpler for customers and BFS

Problem hook

Customers cannot clearly tell whether a plan covers the full treatment cost, a single invoice, a co-payment, or a remaining balance.

Value proposition

One clear pre-confirmation summary explains the financed amount, exclusions, monthly payment, duration, and total repayment.

Data-backed hypothesis

Because 14.6% of installment agreements have post-creation manual contact, we believe a clearer financing-basis summary can reduce manual contacts while protecting activation and payment health.

Slide 6 · Research

Prove confusion is the contact driver

What we know

Responsible Payers rely on informal handoffs, phone calls, emails, forwarded links, invoices, treatment plans and partner explanations.

What we still must prove

Financing-basis confusion must be shown as a top post-creation contact reason before this becomes a scaled product bet.

Control vs variant

Control: current confirmation experience. Variant: add one Financing Basis Summary screen before confirmation.

Decision rule

Ship only if contact-rate reduction is significant, guardrails remain healthy, and diagnostic evidence supports clarity as the driver.

  • Guardrails: activation must not drop by more than 1pp; payment health must not worsen.
  • Diagnostic metric: correct understanding rate and contact reasons tagged as financing-basis confusion.
  • Do not scale unless contact-reason data confirms financing-basis confusion as a meaningful driver.

Slide 10 · Story

AI accelerated the work. Judgment made it useful.

Friction + aha moment

The hardest part was separating real evidence from attractive AI-generated structure. The aha moment: the best first move is not the biggest platform solution, but a focused test of whether Responsible Payers understand what they are committing to.

Key takeaway / next

Good product work is less about generating features and more about making assumptions testable. Next, prove whether financing-basis confusion is a top contact driver with contact-reason tagging before scaling the product bet.
